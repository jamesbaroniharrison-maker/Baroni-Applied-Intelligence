CASE 03 · CONSTRUCTION TENDER READERSTAGE 1

Reads a whole construction tender pack and tells you if it's worth bidding.

Problem

Tender packs are a stack of PDFs, spreadsheets and scans. Miss one requirement and the whole bid's wasted.

What it does

It reads every page, pulls out the deadlines, requirements and risks, and quotes the page for every answer. If it can't find something, it says so.

Result

On a real council tender it caught £2m of cover against £5m required. That's a no bid, caught before anyone lost a week to it.

  1. Pack in
  2. Reads every page
  3. Every answer quoted
  4. Go or no-go
