Before you book

Eight questions, one tap each.

Q1

What can AI automate in a small business?

Tap for the answer
A1

The repetitive, rules-based jobs: client emails, social posts, weekly reports, chasing quotes. On the call we go through your week and start with the biggest one. If nothing's worth automating, I'll tell you straight.

Q2

How much does AI automation cost?

Tap for the answer
A2

It depends on the job, so it's priced per task. After the free call you get a quote for that one task and the monthly running cost, before anything gets built.

Q3

Is my data safe, and is it GDPR compliant?

Tap for the answer
A3

Wherever possible it runs inside tools you already use, like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. Before we build, we agree what data it uses, where it's stored and who can see it.

Q4

How much of my time does an AI automation project take?

Tap for the answer
A4

Not much. A kickoff, a few short check-ins, and approving the work at the review step. You review, I build.

Q5

What support do I get once it's live?

Tap for the answer
A5

Every build comes with a dashboard and a proper handover, and I stick around for fixes and tweaks while it beds in. Ongoing support's there if you want it, with no long contract.

Q6

Do I need to be technical to use AI automation?

Tap for the answer
A6

No. You use it wherever you already work: email, Slack, a simple approval page. Everything underneath is on me.

Q7

How is AI workflow automation different from using ChatGPT myself?

Tap for the answer
A7

ChatGPT waits for you to ask it something. A proper system just runs. It starts from something real happening, drafts in your voice, waits for your OK, sends it and reports back. You're not doing the task faster. You're not doing it at all.

Q8

Tap for the answer
A8

Croydon, South London. Most of the work's done remotely, so I work with small businesses all over the UK.
