BUILD 03 · TENDERIn progress · stage 1

Reads a whole tender pack and tells you if it's worth bidding.

Tender packs are huge. Dozens of documents, and someone has to read every page before they even know if it's worth bidding. So I built something that reads the lot: PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, even scans. It pulls out the deadline, what you have to send back, the requirements and the contract risks, then checks them against your company and gives you a go or no-go. The bit I care about most is that every answer comes with the page and the exact quote it came from, and the value is re-read from that quote, not taken on the AI's word. If it can't find something, it says so. It doesn't guess. I tested it on real council tenders against a demo contractor, and it caught £2m of insurance cover against £5m required and flagged it as a hard stop, before anyone wasted a week on the bid. This is stage one. Next up is the bid writer, which checks every claim in the answer against the company's own records.
