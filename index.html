BUILD 03 · ZOSSIMASolo · end-to-end

One coach that adapts to how you like to be pushed.

One coach that covers fitness, headspace and journaling, and it actually learns your tone across all of it: more direct here, gentler there, depending on what's actually landing for you. Journaling is what closes the loop. Flag a pattern once, say late-night Instagram scrolling your ex's page leaves you feeling like crap, it can just lock that app past a set time. No extra app, no extra step. It also runs a lightweight contact list: flag the people you actually mean to keep in touch with, and it nudges you when it's been too long. A weekly check-in stacks what you planned against what you actually did, without turning it into some scorecard. Built on a Psychology background (BSc, University of Sussex) - the tone-adaptation isn't a prompt trick, it's designed around how people actually respond to feedback.
